What is lead fraud & junk — and why you're paying for it
Foundation · 5 min read
›
🎬 Video walkthrough coming soon

Every click on your ad costs money. But 15–30% of leads from paid campaigns are never real buyers — bots, competitors checking prices, form-spam, duplicate entries and accidental clicks.

  • Bots & crawlers — automated scripts filling your forms thousands of times.
  • Competitor clicks — rivals draining your budget by clicking your ads and submitting junk.
  • Unqualified traffic — people outside your service area, or just browsing with no intent.
  • Duplicates — the same person submitting the form twice inflates CPL and confuses your sales team.

AdGuard scores every lead in real time so your team only calls humans who matter — and the evidence file tells you exactly what to claim back from Google.

Connecting your Google & Meta accounts
Setup · 5 min
›
🎬 Video walkthrough coming soon
  • Open your workspace → Connections tab (next to Dashboard).
  • Click Connect Google Ads → sign in with the Google account that manages your campaigns → allow read + lead access.
  • Click Connect Meta Ads → log into Facebook → pick your ad account → approve lead access.
  • Pick which campaigns should go LIVE for screening — only active campaigns are scored.

OAuth only — your ad platform passwords are never seen or stored by AdGuard.

Reading your lead scores (green / grey / red)
Daily use · 7 min
›
🎬 Video walkthrough coming soon
  • 🟢 Green (verified) — clean, human, ready for your sales team. Delivered to CRM instantly.
  • ⚪ Grey (review) — suspicious signals but not conclusive; check on your Dashboard screen.
  • 🔴 Red (junk) — bot patterns, duplicate or fraudulent; blocked and logged with evidence.

Your Dashboard shows the live stream, junk rate and money saved. Reports gives weekly trends per campaign.

Money Shield thresholds explained
Protection · 6 min
›
🎬 Video walkthrough coming soon
  • Monitor (default) — AdGuard watches junk rates on every campaign and flags breaches. Nothing is paused automatically. Safe way to start.
  • Protect — when a campaign's junk rate crosses your threshold (default 40%), AdGuard auto-pauses it and emails you instantly. Turn this on once you trust the scores.
  • Set both under Settings → Protection Mode. Alerts go to the emails configured in Settings.

Installing the Script Tag (stop bots at the submit button)
Protection · 10 min
›
🎬 Video walkthrough coming soon
In your workspace left panel, click ⚡ Script Tag and copy the snippet.
Paste it into the <head> (or before </body>) of every landing page that collects leads.
WordPress/Elementor: use the "Custom Code" or header scripts field. Shopify: theme.liquid. Webflow: Site Settings → Custom Code.
Submit a test form yourself — the Interceptor verdict appears within ~2 seconds, fail-open (your forms keep working even if the tag ever fails).
Open Script Tag →

CRM delivery setup (LeadSquared, Zoho, HubSpot, webhook)
Delivery · 8 min
›
🎬 Video walkthrough coming soon
  • Go to Settings → Deliver to a CRM → pick yours from the grid (16+ CRMs listed).

Claiming click refunds from Google (evidence file)
Advanced · 10 min
