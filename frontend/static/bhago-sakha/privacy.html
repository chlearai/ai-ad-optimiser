Bhago Sakha

Privacy and Google data use

Updated 12 November 2026

Purpose and access

Bhago Sakha is a private workflow configured for Bhago Mobility's lead calling and payment reminders. Its Google OAuth connection requests spreadsheet read/write access, read-only Drive file metadata for editing-permission checks, and account identity information to confirm the authorised Google account. The program restricts writes to saved Bhago source and test files. It adds row identifiers and approved result columns, preserves the original source fields, and matches outcomes back to the original rows.

Data processed

The calling workflow processes names, mobile numbers, city or area, source dates and, for payment reminders, vehicle numbers and outstanding amounts. Call results can include call identifiers, timestamps, statuses, duration, outcome, summary, callback details, recording links, interest levels and payment commitments. Attempts 1 and 2 are stored separately. A statement that a rider has already paid is not treated as verified payment. Google account email identifies the authorised connection. Offline authorisation tokens allow the worker to access the configured sheets without a browser sign-in for every run.

Use and service providers

Required calling fields are placed into campaign CSV files and uploaded to Samvaad for the configured calls. The worker and its persistent files run on Railway. Connection credentials are encrypted in the application database; the private worker uses its configured encryption key to read them. Google data is used for the configured calling workflow, not sold or used for advertising.

Bhago Sakha's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stored files and control

Campaign CSV files and execution records remain available to support the calling workflow. You can revoke the Google connection through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorised reads; it does not automatically remove previously generated campaign files or records in Samvaad.
