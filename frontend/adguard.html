How the Integrity Gatekeeper works

Every Google Ads lead form submission is scored out of 100: email valid +25 (disposable domains blocked) · phone valid +30 · geo match +15 · Gemini legitimacy up to +30. Leads scoring ≥ 70 with no hard flags are verified and pushed to LeadSquared. Everything else is flagged and kept here only — never pushed to the CRM.

Send a POST with JSON body. Include the shared token in the X-AdGuard-Token header or ?token= query param. The token is configured via the ADGUARD_WEBHOOK_VERIFY_TOKEN env var (default for local dev: adguard_verify).

Recognised fields (flexible key names): name, email, phone, city, state, country, postal_code, campaign_name, gclid, form_id, message. Add account_id or account_name to route the lead to a specific client for LSQ credentials and geo checks.
