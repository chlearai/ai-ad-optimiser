4 LAYERS · ONE SCRIPT TAG

Stop the waste before the money moves.

Add one line of code to your landing page and connect your ad accounts. Setup takes about 15 minutes.

01

Intercept

When someone clicks submit, AdGuard holds it for about 2 seconds. Headless browsers, datacenter IPs, copy-paste bots and repeat offenders are caught before the lead exists.

02

Verify

Borderline leads get a quick WhatsApp OTP. Genuine leads get an AI call within 5 minutes that confirms the person and their intent.

03

Teach

Only verified leads are reported to Meta and Google, with a value for how good they are. The algorithms learn to find buyers, not bots.

04

Exclude

Every bot and fake contact is added to exclusion audiences on all your campaigns. They never see your ads again, so you never pay for them again.

Works with landing-page forms on any website builder. Meta instant forms are protected through Verify, Teach and Exclude.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Built for the layer your forms can't see.

Email format and phone digit checks are table stakes. AdGuard works on the human behind the click.

Pre-Submit Interceptor

Reads device, network and typing behaviour from the moment the page loads. Bots are stopped at the submit button, and they never see an error to learn from.

Conversion Firewall

Nothing reaches Meta or Google until AdGuard approves it. Verified, qualified and site-visit leads are sent with rising values, so bidding favours real buyers.

AI Call Verification

An AI voice agent calls every genuine lead within 5 minutes to confirm who they are and what they want. Your team calls only confirmed prospects.

Exclusion Audiences

Bots and fake contacts are pushed to exclusion lists on every live campaign, including new ones, automatically.

FraudGraph Network

A bot caught on one AdGuard account is flagged for all of them. Code can be copied; network data can't.

Intent Scoring

AI reads each enquiry for buying intent, so "just checking price" and "admission this month" are not treated the same.

Bot Traps

Low-budget decoy ad sets attract bots on purpose, fingerprint them, and block them before they reach your real campaigns.

Proof in Rupees

Junk rate and money saved per campaign and channel, e.g. "YouTube: 62% junk, Search: 8%". Plus evidence packs for Google invalid-click credit requests.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions marketers ask us

Will AdGuard block my real customers? +
Rarely, and never silently on a hunch. Clear bots are blocked; borderline leads get a quick WhatsApp OTP instead of a block. Shadow Mode lets you check our judgement on your own traffic before anything is blocked.
Will it slow down my landing page? +
No. The script loads after your page and form are ready. If AdGuard ever takes longer than 2 seconds, the form submits anyway.
Do I need a developer? +
Only to paste one script tag, the same way you added the Meta pixel. Ad accounts connect with one click.
Does it work with Meta instant forms? +
Yes, for AI verification, the Conversion Firewall and exclusion audiences. The Pre-Submit Interceptor needs a form on your own landing page.
Will reporting fewer conversions hurt my campaigns? +
It changes what the algorithm learns from. You report fewer but real conversions, with values, so bidding shifts toward buyers. We set a minimum signal volume so campaigns keep learning.
What does the AI call sound like? +
A short, polite call confirming the person's name, that they enquired, and one or two questions you choose.
Is my data shared with other businesses? +
No. Only hashed fingerprints of confirmed bots feed the shared network. No one sees your leads or knows you flagged someone.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Monthly plans, no lock-in.
