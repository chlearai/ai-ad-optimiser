🕎 Setup Guide

Connect your accounts + get your first scored lead in under 10 minutes. Tick steps off as you go.

0 of 8 done
1
Open your Connections tab
In your workspace, click Connections — the third tab next to Dashboard and Reports. This is where every ad platform is linked.
Open Connections →
2
Connect Google Ads
Click Connect Google Ads → sign in with the Google account that manages your campaigns → allow access. Takes ~60 seconds.
📷 Screenshot coming soon
3
Connect Meta Ads
Click Connect Meta Ads → log into the Facebook profile tied to your Business Manager → pick your ad account → approve lead access.
📷 Screenshot coming soon
4
Choose campaigns to screen (Go Live)
Tick the campaigns you want scored. Only active campaigns listed as LIVE are screened and delivered to CRM.
5
(Recommended) Install the Script Tag
Left panel → ⚡ Script Tag → copy the one-line snippet → paste into the <head> of every landing page. This stops bot submissions at the form itself.
Open Script Tag →
6
Wait for your first leads
Your Dashboard live stream starts scoring leads in real time — green (verified), grey (review), red (junk). New leads usually appear within minutes of connecting.
7
Set alert emails
Settings → Alert & Report Emails → add up to 5 addresses. Weekly reports (Mondays) + shield alerts go there.
Open Settings →
8
(Optional) Connect your CRM
Settings → Deliver to a CRM → pick from the grid (LeadSquared, Zoho, HubSpot, 16+ more) → paste credentials → Test Connection. Verified leads flow in real time.
Set CRM Delivery →
🎉 All done! Your shield is live — every lead from your connected campaigns is now scored before it reaches your team.
