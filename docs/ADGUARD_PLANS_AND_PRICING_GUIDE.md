## 4. Technical Quota Enforcement & Upgrade Mechanism

### Backend Architecture
- In the `AdGuardAccount` model (`backend/db/models.py`), each workspace tracks:
  - `plan`: `"trial"` | `"starter"` | `"pro"` | `"agency"`
  - `lead_quota`: `100` (trial), `1000` (starter), `5000` (pro), `-1` (agency / unlimited)
  - `lead_count`: Total leads ingested in the billing cycle.

### Quota Checks During Lead Ingestion
1. Whenever a lead arrives via Google webhook or Meta poller (`backend/services/adguard.py`), `process_incoming_lead()` verifies:
   - If `lead_quota > 0` and `lead_count >= lead_quota`, the system flags quota exhaustion and halts CRM pushes until upgraded.
2. At **80% and 95% quota utilization**, automated UI alerts notify the subscriber in the dashboard.

### Upgrade Flow
1. **Self-Serve In-App Upgrade**: Subscribers click **"⚡ Upgrade Plan"** in the sidebar widget or alert banner.
2. **Order Creation (`POST /api/billing/create-order`)**: Backend calculates the upgrade amount in paise and initiates a Razorpay order.
3. **Sandbox Simulation vs. Live Payment**:
   - In **Test/Sandbox Mode**, customers or testing teams can simulate instant Card (`4111 •••• 1111`) or UPI QR Code payment without spending real money.
   - On verification (`POST /api/billing/verify-payment` or `POST /api/billing/sandbox-simulate-success`), the workspace `plan` and `lead_quota` immediately update in the database with an activity log entry, and the UI re-renders in real-time.

AdGuard evaluates 14 deep integrity vectors and shared network threat intelligence that ad platforms cannot see."*

> **Sales Response**: *"Starter is great for baseline volume. However, Pro gives you **Gemini AI Deep Intent Scoring** (which separates genuine buyers from tire-kickers) and the **FraudGraph Shared Threat Network**. With FraudGraph, any scam pattern caught across other high-volume advertisers in India is pre-blocked on your account before your budget is drained."*
